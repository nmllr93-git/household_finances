## App Privacy
- **Privacy Policy URL:** https://larenest.app/privacy
- **Data collection:** **"No, we do not collect data from this app."**
  - Apple counts data as *collected* when it leaves the device to the developer or a partner. Larenest has no server, account or analytics, and the developer never receives anything.
  - Data either stays on the phone (phone-only mode) or goes to the user's **own** computer.
  - Bank sync (SimpleFIN) and market data (the user's own free API keys) go straight from the device to services the user chose. Only stock symbols or a VIN go to the market services.
  - Feedback is an email the user writes and sends themselves.

## Notes
- **EULA:** Apple's standard EULA covers apps with no purchases, so a custom one isn't needed. A custom EULA would only be worth it to add something Apple's doesn't cover.
- **A future Pro tier on iPhone:** Apple's rules (App Review Guideline 3.1.1) say features unlocked inside an iOS app must be sold through Apple In-App Purchase. A license key bought on the website can't unlock features in the iPhone app unless the same unlock is also sold through In-App Purchase (the "multiplatform" exception, 3.1.3(b)). Plan for this before Pro launches.
